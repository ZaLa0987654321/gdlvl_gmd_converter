About Geometry Dash Level Converter (.gdlvl <-> .gmd)

Welcome to the free online tool for converting Geometry Dash level files. This service allows you to quickly and safely convert files from .gdlvl to .gmd format and vice versa without any data loss.

Why is this needed?

The .gdlvl format is a new level import/export format introduced in Geometry Dash 2.209. If you use GDShare and run a GD version older than 2.209, you can use this converter to turn your .gdlvl files into .gmd and import them into your game. You can also convert your .gmd files to .gdlvl to open them in GD version 2.209 or higher.

How to use the converter?

  1. Choose the conversion type you need: from .gdlvl to .gmd or vice versa.
  2. Click the "Choose File" button and upload your level file.
  3. Click the "Download" button to save the converted file to your device.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is it safe?
Yes, all file processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your level files are never uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy.

Why is the download button disabled?
The "Download" button only becomes active after you select and successfully process a valid level file in the corresponding field.
